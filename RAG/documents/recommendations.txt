Business Recommendations

1. Reduce High Discounts

Large discounts significantly reduce profit margins.
Implement discount limits where possible.

2. Focus on Technology Products

Technology products consistently generate strong profits.
Increase inventory and marketing efforts in this category.

3. Promote High-Margin Products

Identify and prioritize products with high profitability.

4. Monitor Discount Policies

Review discount campaigns regularly to ensure they are generating positive returns.

5. Improve Low-Performing Categories

Analyze categories with weak profit performance and adjust pricing or product strategy.

6. Regional Expansion

Expand successful strategies used in high-performing regions to lower-performing regions.

7. Data-Driven Decision Making

Use predictive models to estimate profit before launching promotions or pricing changes.

Conclusion:

A balanced pricing strategy combined with targeted product promotion can significantly improve overall business profitability.
